# Copy Notes

ClambHook official binaries are distributed through GitHub Releases after a
protected publication and described on `clambercloud.com`. Subscriptions and
license device management happen on `store.swiphtgroup.com`.

## Commercial Facts

- Public downloads, once published and independently verified, are linked from
  `https://clambercloud.com/clambhook/download/`.
- Public macOS support is Apple Silicon, macOS 14.0 or later.
- Public GNU/Linux packages target Ubuntu 24.04 LTS and Fedora Linux 44 on
  x86_64 and aarch64; public Android packages target Android 12/API 31+ ARM64.
- New installations receive a 7-day trial; already-started legacy trials keep their original month-long end date.
- A recurring USD 79.99 annual subscription includes releases published during each paid term.
- Signed compatibility entitlements retain `org.jpfchang.clambhook.unlock.lifetime` for the first term and `org.jpfchang.clambhook.feature_update` for later terms.
- Compatible versions released during paid terms remain usable perpetually after cancellation or lapse.
- One key covers a maximum of 6 concurrently active devices across supported platforms.
- License seats can be deactivated and moved to another device.
- Cancellation stops future billing at the paid-through cutoff; resubscription can reuse the same key.
- Verified paid terms grant Copper, Silver, and Gold supporter tiers at one, two, and three or more non-revoked terms.
- ClambHook purchase payments are accepted only through Creem or NOWPayments, not PayPal.
- HTTP(S) capture is public for macOS v1 only in the current website marketing
  contract, as a local opt-in workflow with explicit HTTPS CA trust. The shared
  JavaFX client implements the same local developer surface for GNU/Linux and
  Android; advertise it only for platform assets that have passed the
  publication gate.
